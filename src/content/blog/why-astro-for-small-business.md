  If you're running a small business, your website is often your most valuable asset. But building a fast, professional site doesn't have to break the bank or overwhelm you with technical complexity.

  ## Why Astro?

  Astro is designed with content-focused sites in mind — exactly what most small businesses need. Here's why it's perfect for you:

  ### Zero JavaScript by Default

  Unlike other frameworks that ship tons of JavaScript, Astro only sends the code your page actually needs. This means:

  * **Lightning-fast loading** — your customers won't wait
  * **Better SEO** — search engines can crawl your content immediately
  * **Lower hosting costs** — less bandwidth to serve

  ### Easy to Use

  Write your content in Markdown or HTML — no JavaScript experience needed. Add components where you need interactivity, and Astro handles the rest.

  ### Perfect for Static Hosting

  Deploy anywhere — Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, Netlify. No server setup, no maintenance, just push and go.

  ### Cost-Effective

  Build once, deploy everywhere. Your site stays fast no matter how much traffic you get.

  ## Real-World Results

  I've built dozens of small business sites using Astro, and the results speak for themselves:

  - **Faster conversions** — visitors stay longer and take action
  - **Higher rankings** — Google loves fast sites
  - **Lower maintenance** — no server updates or security patches
  - **Peace of mind** — your site just works, all the time
